Everything you need to know about booking lab tests at home in Malebennur

Booking a blood test at home in Malebennur is effortless with Janatha Clinical Laboratory:
1. Search and select your required tests or comprehensive health checkup packages on this page.
2. Provide your address and select your preferred time slot (blood collection within 15 minutes or schedule for later).
3. Complete checkout via online payment or select Pay on Sample Collection.
Our trained and vaccinated eMedic will arrive at your home on time for sterile sample collection.

Our eMedics collect samples 7 days a week, from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM across all major localities in Malebennur and nearby villages. You can book an early morning fasting slot starting as early as 6:00 AM, or an urgent same-day sample collection whenever needed.

For most routine blood tests (CBC, Thyroid, Lipid Profile, LFT, KFT, HbA1c, Dengue, Urine Routine), reports are delivered within 6 to 10 hours of sample collection. You will receive an instant WhatsApp notification with a direct PDF download link and an email copy.

Fasting depends on the specific tests booked. For lipid profiles, fasting blood sugar, and certain full-body packages, 10 to 12 hours of overnight fasting is required (water is permitted). For CBC, Thyroid, Dengue, HbA1c, and Vitamin tests, fasting is not required. Each test card clearly displays fasting requisites before booking.

We use gold-standard automated clinical chemistry and hematology analyzers from Roche, Abbott, and Beckman Coulter. Reports are delivered digitally with clear reference ranges, and samples are transported in strict 0°C to 4°C temperature-monitored cold chain boxes to preserve specimen integrity.

Our eMedics transport collected samples inside certified temperature-controlled cold-chain boxes maintained strictly between 0°C to 4°C with digital temperature loggers. This prevents specimen degradation and hemolysis, ensuring test accuracy identical to a hospital lab visit.
